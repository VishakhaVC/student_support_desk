# Northbridge Institute of Management — Student Handbook (SAMPLE)

> FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DATA. This handbook was written for a college assignment. It is NOT an official policy of any real institution.

## 1. Tuition Fees

### 1.1 Fee structure
Tuition fees are charged per trimester. The programme fee for each trimester is shown on the student's fee record in the student portal. Fees are payable in up to two instalments per trimester unless the Finance Office approves a different plan in writing.

### 1.2 Payment deadlines
The first instalment is due before the start of the trimester. The second instalment is due on the date shown in the fee record, normally within eight weeks of the trimester start. A late fee of 2% of the overdue amount applies for each week the payment is late, up to a maximum of 6%.

### 1.3 Accepted payment methods
Payments can be made through the student payment portal using net banking, UPI or debit card, or by demand draft at the Finance Office. Cash payments are not accepted. The institute never asks students for their card details, passwords or one-time passwords over chat, phone or email.

## 2. Payment Portal Failures

### 2.1 When the payment portal fails
If the payment portal fails or the transaction is declined while money has been debited, the student must report the problem to the Finance Office within 24 hours. The report must include the transaction reference number and a screenshot of the failure or the bank debit message.

### 2.2 Next working day payment
When a portal failure has been reported within 24 hours with the required proof, the student may complete the payment on the next working day. No late fee is charged for that delay. If the failure is not reported within 24 hours, the normal late fee rules in section 1.2 apply.

### 2.3 Duplicate or failed debits
If money was debited but the payment does not appear on the fee record after three working days, the student should raise a support request in the category "payment" so that the Finance Office can reconcile the transaction with the bank.

## 3. Scholarships

### 3.1 Types of scholarship
The institute offers a Merit Scholarship, a Need-Based Scholarship and a Sports Excellence Scholarship. Each scholarship reduces the tuition fee by a fixed percentage shown on the student's scholarship record.

### 3.2 Eligibility conditions
The Merit Scholarship requires a trimester CGPA of 3.0 or above and no academic misconduct case. The Need-Based Scholarship requires a family income declaration reviewed by the Scholarship Committee. A student may hold only one scholarship at a time.

### 3.3 Renewal and review
Scholarships are reviewed at the end of every trimester. A scholarship is suspended if the CGPA falls below the condition for that scholarship, and it can be restored after the next review if the condition is met again. Scholarship decisions are made by the Scholarship Committee, not by the portal or the AI assistant.

## 4. Course Changes

### 4.1 Course change window
A student may request a change of elective course within the first two weeks of the trimester. Requests made after the window need the written approval of the Programme Director.

### 4.2 How to request a course change
Course change requests are submitted by raising a support request in the category "course_change". The request must name the current course, the requested course and the reason. Approval depends on seat availability and prerequisite completion. Core courses cannot be changed.

## 5. Academic Support

### 5.1 Academic support services
Students who need help with coursework can book a session with the Academic Support Centre. Peer tutoring and writing-skills workshops are available during the trimester. Students on academic warning are contacted by their programme coordinator.

## 6. Student Support Requests and Escalation

### 6.1 Support requests
Students raise a support request in the student portal, choosing a category and describing the issue. Each request receives a reference number. The Student Services team aims to respond within two working days.

### 6.2 Escalation procedure
If a support request is not answered within five working days, the student may ask for escalation to the Student Services Manager. Urgent fee problems may be escalated to the Finance Office directly.

### 6.3 Changes to registered contact details
A change to a registered email address or phone number needs verification by Student Services before it is applied. Students can ask for the change through the portal or the assistant, and the change takes effect only after staff approval.
